Slide 2: Problem Statement

Disaster Logistics & Safety Hazards

Manual drone setup delays ambulance support and critical emergency response when every second counts. In disaster zones, responders cannot afford time lost to deployment checks and ground prep. Forced ground landings in unstructured debris cause high propeller strike rates and crashes.

Inflexible Single-Purpose Fleet Costs

Traditional UAVs feature fixed, non-swappable payloads. Buying separate fleets for search-and-rescue, medical drops, and utility maintenance can raise hardware expenditure by over 60%.

Solar Maintenance Efficiency & Resource Waste

Dust buildup reduces solar output by 15–30% monthly. Scheduled water cleaning wastes large amounts of water, even when light dust could be cleared with compressed air.

Slide 3: Proposed Solution

  • Emergency-First ML Dispatch Engine: Integrates with 108/112 systems to auto-select and auto-latch mission payloads.
  • Universal Hover-Winch Assembly: Tension-sensing winch lowers payload safely from hover in flooded or rubble terrain.
  • Conductive Fast-Charging Base Station: Self-aligning conductive pad enables recharge and automated payload swaps for 24/7 operation.

Modular Mission Payload Suite

  • AED & First-Aid Module: Rapid medical supply delivery.
  • Emergency Rations Module: Precision drops of food and water.
  • Victim Detection Module: ESP32 BLE RSSI + onboard AI vision for victim tracking.
  • Solar Cleaning Module: Dust-density sensing switches between air and targeted water spray.

Slide 4: Innovation & Uniqueness

  • First multi-role platform combining emergency logistics and solar utility maintenance on one chassis.
  • Hover-tether winch delivery reduces propeller strike hazards on uneven ground.
  • Real-time dust-density detection can reduce water usage by up to 80%.
  • Fast-charging + auto payload latching supports autonomous 24/7 deployment.
  • One drone. Every mission. Zero downtime.

Slide 5: Target Users & Market

  • Emergency Response Agencies: Faster aid delivery for ambulance-delay and remote incident scenarios.
  • Solar Energy Operators: Utility-scale maintenance use cases keep fleet active and mission-ready.
  • Hybrid Organizations: Shared fleet model maximizes ROI across emergency + renewable operations.

Addressable Market: India’s disaster response sector + 500+ GW solar expansion creates a large, high-growth opportunity.

Slide 6: Feasibility

Technical Stack

  • Pixhawk flight controller with optical precision landing
  • ROS / ESP32 base-station interface
  • NEMA stepper winch with high-tensile Kevlar tether
  • High-current conductive charging plates with hydrophobic sealed enclosure

Key Challenges & Mitigation

  • Center-of-gravity balancing during fluid dropouts
  • Weatherproofing exposed conductive contacts
