SKYFOX

Autonomous Multi-Role Drone System for Emergency Response & Solar Infrastructure.

Presented by Team RedFox Open Technical Notes
02 / The problem

When every second matters, setup time costs lives.

Disaster zones are unpredictable. Ground access disappears, landing areas fill with rubble, and a single-purpose fleet cannot keep up with changing needs.

01
Slow emergency deployment

Manual setup delays ambulance support and critical aid.

02
Unsafe ground landings

Debris and uneven terrain cause propeller strikes and crashes.

03
Expensive fixed fleets

Different payloads require different drones and more hardware.

03 / Why now

One emergency. Three broken workflows.

Existing solutions solve only one part of the response chain. Skyfox joins delivery, detection, and infrastructure care in one modular platform.

Traditional UAV fleet

  • ✕ Fixed, single-purpose payloads
  • ✕ Needs safe ground landing
  • ✕ Manual payload changes
  • ✕ Idle between missions

Skyfox Project

  • ✓ Quick-swap mission payloads
  • ✓ Hover-winch delivery
  • ✓ Auto-recharge + relaunch
  • ✓ Emergency + solar in one chassis
04 / Our solution

Meet the mission-adaptive aircraft.

Skyfox is a Multi-Purpose airframe with an automated payload rack, universal hover-winch, onboard sensing, and a conductive fast-charging base station.

✚

Medical aid

AED and first-aid delivery from hover.

⌁

Victim detection

BLE RSSI + thermal + sonar modules for search.

◒

Solar cleaning

Dust sensing chooses air or water.

▣

Emergency rations

Precision food and water drops.

05 / How it works

From alert to action in four moves.

01

Dispatch

Emergency system selects the mission and payload.

02

Launch

Skyfox self-checks, latches, and leaves the base.

03

Deliver

Hover-winch lowers aid safely above rough terrain.

04

Reset

Return, conductive charge, swap, and relaunch.

06 / What makes it different

07 / Feasibility

Built with available technology.

Technical stack

  • Pixhawk flight controller
  • ROS + ESP32 base station interface
  • NEMA stepper winch + Kevlar tether
  • Optical precision landing
  • Sealed conductive charging plates

Prototype plan

1–4Airframe + flight
5–9Payload + winch
10–15Base + field test

Estimated full-suite prototype cost: ₹1L–₹1.5L
