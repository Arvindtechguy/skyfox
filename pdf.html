SkyFox: Autonomous Multi-Role UAV Platform

Sub-title: AI Dispatch, Modular Winch Payloads & Conductive Fast-Docking Station

Event: MECHAESTA’26 Ideathon | Domain: Robotics, Drones & Smart Automation | Team: RedFox

Team Members: Arvind Narayan M R, M Dharnesh Bala, Govarthan M, Shri Nivas S

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

When every second matters, should emergency response wait for humans—or should technology be ready to respond? SkyFox bridges the critical delay window during emergency ambulance transit and post-disaster operations by integrating multi-role capabilities onto a single heavy-lift hexacopter platform, saving over 60% in fleet procurement costs.

  • Disaster Logistics Delays & Hazards: Manual prep wastes critical response time; forced landings on disaster debris cause rotor strikes and airframe crashes.
  • Single-Purpose Fleet Costs: Dedicated drones for search, drops, and cleaning bloat capital expenditure by 60%+.
  • Water Waste in Solar O&M: Scheduled routine water cleaning wastes thousands of liters on dust that can be cleared with dry air.

2. Proposed Solution & Core Modules

  • ML-Driven Dispatch Engine: Integrates helpline feeds (108/112) to auto-select and mechanically latch payload pods without human intervention.
  • Hover-Tether Winch: Employs sonar-guided tether lowering from a 5–10 m hover, bypassing jagged ground debris entirely.
  • Conductive Fast-Docking: High-current (24V/15A) copper charging plates enable 24/7 unassisted autonomous cycles.

3. Modular Mission Payload Suite

  • AED & Emergency Medical Pod: Rapid trauma kits and defibrillator drops in inaccessible disaster zones.
  • Disaster Rations Pod: Winch-lowered emergency food, water, and survival packs to stranded survivors.
  • Multi-Spectral Survivor Detection Pod: Radiometric FLIR Lepton LWIR thermal, Sony 4K RGB, ESP32 BLE RSSI triangulation, and MaxBotix sonar to locate buried victims.
  • Dual-Mode Solar Maintenance Pod: Optical dust density camera intelligently selecting dry compressed air or micro-water spray (saving up to 80% water).
